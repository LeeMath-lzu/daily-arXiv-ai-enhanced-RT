Result: 得到区间秩的显式公式与本质覆盖定理；替换构造保持相应区间秩不变量，并在线段上恢复通常秩不变量；给出两压缩系统产生同一不变量的充分条件；全压缩时与 Kim--Mémoli 广义秩不变量一致，二维网格上恢复 Dey--Kim--Mémoli 的 zigzag 公式。

Conclusion: 区间替换与本质覆盖为任意压缩系统提供统一框架，刻画区间秩不变量并推广已有结果。

Abstract: Persistence modules indexed by finite posets arise in topological data analysis. Unlike in the one-parameter case, they need not decompose into interval modules. Interval replacements approximate them by formal differences of interval decomposable modules. Extending earlier work on 2D-grids, we study these replacements for arbitrary compression systems. Different choices can give different interval rank invariants and replacements, including those related to interval resolutions on commutative ladders. Our main results are an explicit formula for interval ranks and an essential-cover theorem. Using Auslander--Reiten theory, we express interval ranks in terms of ranks of matrices formed from the structure maps of the module. The essential-cover theorem recovers each interval rank from a suitable restriction of the module. The replacement construction preserves the corresponding interval rank invariant, which recovers the usual rank invariant on segments. Essential covers also give a sufficient condition for two compression systems to yield the same invariant. For total compression, the invariant coincides with the generalized rank invariant of Kim and Mémoli. On 2D-grids, essential covers recover the zigzag formula of Dey, Kim, and Mémoli for this invariant.
