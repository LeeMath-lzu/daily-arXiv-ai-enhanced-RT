Result: 闭五顶点模型的状态构成Demazure晶体，强于仅把配分函数识别为Demazure特征标；开模型状态实现（晶体）Demazure原子，并给出独立新证明；闭玻色模型满足Yang-Baxter方程并具有Demazure-Lusztig递推，在t=0时退化为闭五顶点模型的玻色类比且其状态构成Demazure晶体。

Conclusion: 着色五顶点模型为Demazure特征标与Demazure晶体提供了格点模型实现，晶体结构可由格点移动与重新着色显式构造；融合方法可系统地把开模型结果平移为闭的、含参数t的玻色版本，形成统一的Demazure-Lusztig递推框架。

Abstract: We construct and study solvable lattice models whose partition functions are Demazure characters. Two types of colored generalizations of the five-vertex model can be defined that are called the open and closed models. States of both models are associated with Gelfand-Tsetlin patterns, or equivalently, semistandard Young tableaux. We characterize the tableaux arising from states of the closed model and prove that they form a Demazure crystal. This is stronger than identifying the partition function as a Demazure character: it requires constructing Kashiwara crystal operators on the states through lattice moves and recoloring. For the open model, Brubaker, Bump, Buciumas, and Gustafsson showed that the states realize (crystal) Demazure atoms. We also give a new, independent proof of their result.
  Bump and Naprienko considered bosonic models involving a parameter t. In these models, the edges and vertices are constructed by fusion of simpler edge and vertex types. Taking t=0 produces bosonic analogs of the open model. By reversing the fusion order, we construct closed bosonic models and establish their Yang-Baxter equations, yielding a Demazure-Lusztig recursion for the partition functions. At t=0, these models specialize to bosonic analogues of the closed five-vertex model, and we prove that their states form Demazure crystals.
