### [21] [Representations of Coxeter groups over fusion rings and hyperplane complements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04836)
*Edmund Heng,Luis Paris*

Main category: math.GR

TL;DR: 研究了Coxeter群在fusion ring上的忠实实现及其关联的Vinberg系统，证明了它们诱导超平面补集的嵌入，从而给出Artin–Tits群之间某类强容许(LCM)同态的几何实现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 希望为Artin–Tits群之间的强容许(LCM)同态寻找几何解释，并推广Coxeter群表示与Vinberg系统的经典框架。

Method: 在fusion ring（融合环）上构造Coxeter群的忠实表示，并研究相应的Vinberg系统，进而利用这些系统分析超平面补集之间的映射。

Result: 证明Coxeter群在fusion ring上的忠实实现所对应的Vinberg系统能诱导超平面补集的嵌入。

Conclusion: 该类Vinberg系统为某些强容许(LCM)同态提供了几何实现，把表示论构造与Artin–Tits群的几何理论联系起来。

Abstract: We study faithful realisations of Coxeter groups over fusion rings and study Vinberg systems associated to them. We show that they induce embeddings of hyperplane complements, which provide geometrical realisations of certain types of strong admissible (LCM) homomorphisms between Artin--Tits groups.

### [22] [Weyl chambers, asymptotic expansions, and blowup analysis of explicit Toda solutions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30767)
*Zhaohu Nie*

Main category: math.AP

TL;DR: 本文在Lin等人的blowup分析框架下研究[Wie25]构造的径向Toda解族：刻画了Weyl室内点与边界面上Cartan元素的渐近差异，证明u_i^{λ,H}(r)+2log r的一致上界，并用射线H+tq_μ与根超平面的交点确定逐级bubbling尺度，将极限轮廓识别为Levi-Toda子系统的显式解，且这些气泡质量之和等于全部blowup质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Toda系统具有丰富的blowup现象，[Wie25]已构造出blowup质量对应Lie代数Weyl群任意元素的显式径向解族，但缺乏系统的blowup分析。作者希望把这些显式解族纳入C.-S. Lin及其合作者发展的blowup分析框架，理解逐级气泡的尺度与质量分配机制。

Method: 对解族作渐近分析，区分位于Weyl室内部的Cartan元素与位于边界面的情形；证明u_i^{λ,H}(r)+2log r的一致上界以获得以原点为中心的selection估计；利用射线H+tq_μ与各根超平面的交点确定慢衰减下的连续bubbling尺度，在每次穿越处进行rescaling并结合面渐近分析识别极限轮廓；再用[KLNW24]计算各气泡质量。

Result: 证明了u_i^{λ,H}(r)+2log r的一致上界，从而使selection估计在原点处成立；给出bubbling尺度的几何刻画（根超平面交点序列），并证明每个穿越处的极限轮廓为对应Levi-Toda子系统的显式解；通过[KLNW24]计算的质量之和恰好等于整个blowup质量。A_2型例子验证了该识别，并同时展示分离的标量气泡与一次性的全系统气泡。

Conclusion: 径向Toda解族的blowup结构可由Weyl室几何（射线与根超平面的交点）完全刻画：逐级气泡尺度与Levi-Toda极限轮廓一一对应，且质量守恒成立。这说明[Wie25]的显式解族是研究Toda系统blowup的合适模型，也验证了Lin框架在Toda系统上的适用性。

Abstract: Blowup analysis is a central part of geometric analysis, and systems of PDEs such as Toda systems exhibit particularly rich blowup behavior. In [Nie25], the author constructed explicit families of radial Toda solutions whose blowup masses correspond to arbitrary elements of the Weyl group of the underlying Lie algebra. In this paper, we study these families within the framework of blowup analysis developed by C.-S. Lin and his coauthors. The key is an asymptotic analysis of the solution family that distinguishes Cartan elements in the interior of a Weyl chamber from those lying on its boundary faces. We prove a uniform upper bound for $u_i^{λ,H}(r)+2\log r$, so that the selection estimate holds with the center at the origin. We show that the successive bubbling scales for slow decay are determined by the intersections of the ray $H+tq_μ$ with root hyperplanes. At each crossing, rescaling and the face asymptotics identify the limiting profiles as explicit solutions of the corresponding Levi-Toda subsystems. The masses of these successive bubbles, computed using [KLNW24], account for the entire blowup mass. Examples in type $A_2$ demonstrate this identification and exhibit both separated scalar bubbles and a simultaneous full-system bubble.
