### [1] [Mutation of τ-exceptional pairs and sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10301)
*Aslak B. Buan,Eric J. Hanson,Bethany R. Marsh*

Main category: math.RT

TL;DR: 本文引入了任意有限维代数上模的τ-exceptional序列的变异（mutation）概念，并研究了其性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将经典exceptional序列的变异概念推广到更一般的有限维代数框架，并探究其与support τ-tilting模变异之间的联系。

Method: 定义τ-exceptional序列的变异；对hereditary代数验证其与经典exceptional序列变异的一致性；对rank two代数分析变异的传递性。

Result: 对hereditary代数，所定义的变异与经典exceptional序列变异一致；对rank two代数，τ-exceptional序列变异的传递性等价于Adachi-Iyama-Reiten意义下support τ-tilting模变异的传递性。

Conclusion: τ-exceptional序列的变异推广了经典理论，并在rank two情形下与support τ-tilting模的变异传递性建立等价关系。

Abstract: We introduce a notion of mutation for $τ$-exceptional sequences of modules over arbitrary finite dimensional algebras. For hereditary algebras, we show that this coincides with the classical mutation of exceptional sequences. For rank two algebras, we show that mutation of $τ$-exceptional sequences is transitive if and only if mutation of support $τ$-tilting modules in the sense of Adachi-Iyama-Reiten is transitive.

### [2] [Non-Equivalence of Smooth and Nodal Conformal Block Functors in Logarithmic CFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07720)
*Hao Zhang*

Main category: math.QA

TL;DR: 本文证明了对非有理的C2-余有限VOA，模空间上的共形块空间不构成向量丛，共变子层非局部自由，且mode transition algebra与自同态对象不同构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在有理VOA情形，共形块空间给出模空间上的向量丛，但作者希望探究非有理（非最低权生成）情形下这一现象是否成立，以理解非有理情形与有理情形的本质差异。

Method: 考虑N-分次、C2-余有限的VOA，其模范畴中存在非最低权生成的模（如triplet代数与偶辛费米子VOA），比较光滑曲线与节点曲线上共形块空间的维数。

Result: 当N≥4时，模空间上不存在以共形块空间为纤维的向量丛，因为光滑曲线与节点曲线的共形块维数不同；相应共变子层非局部自由。

Conclusion: 与有理情形不同，Damiolini-Gibney-Krashen引入的mode transition algebra不同构于模范畴上的自同态对象，揭示了非有理VOA的模空间几何的独特性。

Abstract: Let $\mathbb V$ be an $\mathbb N$-graded, $C_2$-cofinite vertex operator algebra (VOA) admitting a non-lowest generated module in $\mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb V)$ (e.g., the triplet algebras $\mathcal{W}_p$ for $p\in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 2}$ or the even symplectic fermion VOAs $SF_d^+$ for $d\in \mathbb{Z}_+$). We prove that, unlike in the rational case, for $N\geq 4$ there is no vector bundle on $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,N}$ whose fibers are the spaces of conformal blocks associated to certain $\mathbb{V}$-modules, since the dimensions of these conformal block spaces differ between smooth and nodal curves. Consequently, the sheaf of coinvariants associated to these $\mathbb V$-modules on $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,N}$ is not locally free for $N\geq 4$. It also follows that, unlike in the rational case, the mode transition algebra $\mathfrak A$ introduced by Damiolini-Gibney-Krashen is not isomorphic to the end $\mathbb E=\int_{\mathbb X\in \mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb X)}\mathbb X\otimes \mathbb{X}'$ as an object of $\mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb{V}^{\otimes 2})$.

</details>

### [3] [Cluster algebra and quasimap quantum cohomology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09875)
*Yingchun Zhang,Zijun Zhou*

Main category: math.AG

TL;DR: 本文将阿贝尔化技术应用于GIT商簇的拟映射量子上同调，给出了显式的环表示，并在有向无环箭图的箭图簇上建立了等变拟映射量子上同调环的聚类代数结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: GIT商簇的拟映射量子上同调环缺乏显式的环表示，且其与聚类代数的联系尚不清晰，作者希望用阿贝尔化技术填补这一空白。

Method: 采用阿贝尔化（abelianization）技术，推导GIT商簇拟映射量子上同调的显式环表示；并将该结果应用至有向无环箭图对应的箭图簇。

Result: 得到了GIT商簇拟映射量子上同调的显式环表示；对有向无环箭图的箭图簇，在其等变拟映射量子上同调环上建立了聚类代数结构。

Conclusion: 阿贝尔化技术是研究拟映射量子上同调的有效工具，并揭示了拟映射量子上同调与聚类代数之间的深层联系。

Abstract: We apply the abelianization technique to obtain an explicit ring presentation for the quasimap quantum cohomology of GIT quotients. As an application, for quiver varieties associated with oriented-acyclic quivers, we establish a cluster algebra structure on their equivariant quasimap quantum cohomology rings.

### [4] [Weak del Pezzo surfaces are characterized by the existence of $2$-tilting bundles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26199)
*Ryu Tomonaga*

Main category: math.AG

TL;DR: 本文研究d-tilting丛的存在性，证明了其蕴含反典范丛半丰沛且大（即弱Fano），并刻画了特征任意下弱del Pezzo曲面恰为拥有2-tilting丛的曲面，从而验证并推广了Chan猜想，同时构造了反典范环的NCCR。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将Chan在特征零下提出的猜想（光滑射影曲面拥有2-tilting丛当且仅当为弱del Pezzo曲面）推广到任意特征，并将tilting丛与弱Fano、NCCR等几何性质联系起来。

Method: 通过研究d-tilting丛的自同态代数的整体维数与d-表示tame性，证明其存在性推出反典范丛半丰沛且大；对弱del Pezzo曲面构造2-tilting丛；并利用典范丛全空间上拉回丛的自同态代数构造NCCR。

Result: 存在d-tilting丛蕴含反典范丛半丰沛且大，故簇为弱Fano，且自同态代数为d-表示tame；代数闭域任意特征下弱del Pezzo曲面恰有2-tilting丛；由此验证Chan猜想并推广至任意特征；构造了反典范环的NCCR，得到Du Val del Pezzo曲面反典范锥的NCCR，并证明其为极小消解上2-tilting丛自同态代数的3-Calabi-Yau完备化。

Conclusion: d-tilting丛的存在性刻画了弱Fano性质，并在曲面情形精确对应弱del Pezzo曲面；该框架统一给出了反典范环的NCCR构造，把光滑del Pezzo情形推广到Du Val情形，验证并拓展了Chan猜想。

Abstract: For $d\geq1$, a tilting bundle on a regular proper $d$-dimensional variety over a field is called $d$-tilting if its endomorphism algebra has global dimension $d$, the smallest possible value. We prove that, over any field, the existence of a $d$-tilting bundle implies that the anticanonical bundle is semiample and big, and hence the variety is weak Fano. Moreover, the endomorphism algebra is $d$-representation tame.
  Conversely, every weak del Pezzo surface over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic admits a $2$-tilting bundle. Thus a smooth projective surface over such a field admits a $2$-tilting bundle if and only if it is a weak del Pezzo surface. This proves the variety case of Chan's conjecture, originally formulated in characteristic zero, and extends it to arbitrary characteristic.
  As an application, we construct a non-commutative crepant resolution (NCCR) of the anticanonical ring of any regular proper $d$-dimensional variety over any field admitting a $d$-tilting bundle. The NCCR is the endomorphism algebra of the pulled-back bundle on the total space of the canonical bundle. In particular, we obtain NCCRs of anticanonical cones over Du Val del Pezzo surfaces over algebraically closed fields of arbitrary characteristic. These NCCRs are the $3$-Calabi--Yau completions of the endomorphism algebras of $2$-tilting bundles on the minimal resolutions, extending the known construction for smooth del Pezzo surfaces to the Du Val case.

</details>
