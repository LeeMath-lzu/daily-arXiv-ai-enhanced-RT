Abstract: Let $P$ be a finite connected poset and let $Λ_P$ be the opposite endomorphism algebra of the direct sum of all interval representations of $P$ over a field. Abstract: Finite-dimensional zigzag modules decompose into interval modules. For an ordinary persistence module, every fully supported interval submodule of a restriction splits as a direct summand, whereas this need not hold for a zigzag module. We study this failure of splitting. A breaker pair is a local configuration of two overlapping interval summands with a backward arrow at the left boundary of the second interval and a forward arrow at the right boundary of the first. A single breaker pair gives a local nonsplitting interval submodule, but it need not control a prescribed larger restriction. We therefore introduce breaker chains.
