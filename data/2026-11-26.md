Propagating this gap through the PBW filtration of $\mathrm{U}(\mathrm{Vir})/(z-c)$, for every nonscalar element, we construct an alternating adjoint operator that lowers its order by exactly one.
  As applications, we compute the annihilators of all representations with nonzero central character, prove a strengthened form of Etingof's conjecture on nonsplit extensions of $W$-modules, and reduce the classification of prime ideals of $\mathrm{U}(\mathrm{Vir})$ to those of $\mathrm{U}(W)$.

Motivation: Strassen 曾提出：给定张量，其渐近谱是否为闭区间？此前只在特定张量族或特定谱点族上有结果，一般情形未知。作者希望给出一般性的路径连通性证明，并把方法推广到其他渐近问题（如图与 Shannon 容量）。

Method: 作者发展了谱点之间"插值"的一般方法，适用于在信息论"类型方法"意义下可分解的 Strassen 半环，且其谱点满足受 slice rank 启发的覆盖性质；张量情形下的"类型"由对称群不可约表示分解给出。

Result: 证明了每个谱点都可通过路径连到任一"一腿对其余"展平秩；由此得到渐近谱在任意固定张量上是闭区间。方法还适用于图与 Shannon 容量的情形，与 Vrana (2019) 的插值一致。

Conclusion: 利用插值方法，作者还获得了矩形矩阵乘法、多项式乘法与非交叉（层流）量子泛函的渐近谱方面的新结果及已有结果的更简证明。

Abstract: We prove path-connectedness of the asymptotic spectrum of complex tensors of every fixed order $k \geq 3$: every spectral point is connected by a path to every "one leg versus the rest" flattening rank. In particular, the asymptotic spectrum evaluated at any fixed tensor is a closed interval, answering a question of Strassen. This was previously known only on specific families of tensors (Strassen, FOCS 1986, Crelle 1987-1991; Wigderson and Zuiddam, Bull. AMS 2026) or for specific families of spectral points (Christandl, Vrana and Zuiddam, STOC 2018; Sakabe, Doğan and Walter, FOCS 2026; Alman, Li and Pratt, FOCS 2026).
  To obtain our path-connectedness theorem we develop a general method for "interpolating" between spectral points, which applies to Strassen semirings that are appropriately decomposable (in the sense of an information-theoretic "method of types" on the semiring) and whose spectral points satisfy a cover property (inspired by slice rank). This covers tensors and asymptotic rank (with "types" given by decomposition into symmetric group irreps), as well as other objects and asymptotic problems, like graphs and Shannon capacity, where it matches the interpolation of Vrana (2019).
  Using our interpolation methods we furthermore obtain new results and simpler proofs of previous results regarding the asymptotic spectrum of rectangular matrix multiplication, polynomial multiplication and non-crossing (laminar) quantum functionals.
