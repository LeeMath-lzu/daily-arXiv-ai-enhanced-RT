Motivation: Drinfeld–Jimbo量子包络代数$U_q(\mathfrak{g})$的Borel部分$U_q(\mathfrak{b})$作为Hopf $*$-代数，缺乏解析层面的量子群实现，需要构造相应的局部紧量子群以统一代数与解析框架。

Method: 设$\hbar$为非零实数，$q=e^{-\pi i\hbar}$，$G$为经典型分裂半单实Lie群。作者构造局部紧量子群$(L_\hbar(B),\Delta)$，使其积分$U_q(\mathfrak{b})$；随后计算其Haar权与模数据，并考察其Drinfeld double与全正半群$G^+$函数代数的关系。

Result: 证明了存在局部紧量子群$(L_\hbar(B),\Delta)$积分$U_q(\mathfrak{b})$作为Hopf $*$-代数，并显式给出Haar权与模数据。此外，在多余Cartan部分之外，其Drinfeld double可视为全正半群$G^+$函数代数的解析量子化。

Conclusion: 该工作为量子包络代数的Borel部分提供了局部紧量子群实现，并将Drinfeld double与全正半群$G^+$的解析量子化联系起来。

Abstract: Let $\hbar$ be a non-zero real number and put $q= e^{-πi \hbar}$. Let $G$ be a semisimple split real Lie group of classical type, with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ and associated Borel subalgebra $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq \mathfrak{g}$. We show that there exists a locally compact quantum group $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$ which integrates the Borel part $U_q(\mathfrak{b})$ of the Drinfeld--Jimbo quantized enveloping algebra $U_q(\mathfrak{g})$, viewed as a Hopf $*$-algebra. We further compute the associated Haar weights and modular data for $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$. Up to an excess Cartan part, the Drinfeld double of $(L_{\hbar}(B),Δ)$ can be seen as an analytic quantization of the function algebra of the totally positive semigroup $G^+\subseteq G$.
